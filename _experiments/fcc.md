The FCC is CERN's planned next large-scale collider, aiming to reach unprecedented energies in particle collisions. The project encompasses several collider concepts, including a hadron collider (FCC-hh) and an electron-positron collider (FCC-ee), designed to explore new physics beyond the Standard Model.
